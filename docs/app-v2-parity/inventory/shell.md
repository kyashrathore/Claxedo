**Conventions**
- v1 paths are relative to the old app's `src` (deleted at the swap), unless they start with `packages/`. The kit is `packages/ui/src`.
- v2 paths are relative to `packages/claxedo-app/src`.
- "Measured" means `getComputedStyle` in the running apps (v1 on 4481, v2 on 4480, same daemon), light scheme, 1280×800.
- Screenshots are in `scratchpad/lanes/inventory/shell/shots/`, named `<screen>-<app>-<width>.png`.
- **The owner confirmed two v1 rules on 2026-09-24:**
  - compact tabs appear only while the left sidebar is closed (SHELL-502);
  - files open inside the right panel's own tabs, never as workbench tabs or panes (SHELL-606).
