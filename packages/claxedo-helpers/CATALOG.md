# Helper capability catalog

Generated by `bun script/helpers/catalog.ts` from a full read of every top-level
helper in `packages/`. It answers one question: **what methods would a shared
helpers package have to provide?** Whether each is written here or taken from a
dependency is a separate decision, and replacing call sites is a separate job
after that.

`demand` counts existing definitions; `impls` counts how many of them are
genuinely different code. A capability with demand 9 and impls 9 is nine people
solving one problem nine ways — the strongest case for a single owner, and the
most dangerous to consolidate blind, because some of the differences are
deliberate.

10389 capabilities across 11933 helpers; 1858 are general-purpose.
