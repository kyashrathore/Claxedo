## daniell-taj-dither.webp

A 1-bit ordered dither (8 × 8 Bayer, 560 × 347) of **"The Taje Mahel, Agra, taken in
the garden"**, an etching with aquatint, hand-coloured, drawn and engraved by Thomas
and William Daniell for *Oriental Scenery*, 1801, held by the Library of Congress.

- Source file: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_Taje_Mahel,_Agra._No._I_-_drawn_%26_engraved_by_Thomas_and_William_Daniell._LCCN2003665020.jpg
  (Library of Congress catalog record https://lccn.loc.gov/2003665020; the record is
  titled "No. I", but the print is the garden view, as its engraved caption reads).
  Downloaded as the 3840 × 2637 rendition of the 10257 × 7044 original.
- Licence: public domain in the United States (published before 1929, Commons
  `PD-US-expired`; the Library of Congress lists no known restrictions).
- Made from that file: the plate area cropped out of the paper margin, greyscale,
  autocontrast (1 % cut), a light edge pass (edges found on a 0.8 px blur, ×5,
  subtracted), a tone curve (black 70, white 200, gamma 2.4), smoothstep fades to
  paper over the outer 24 % of each side and over the sky above the dome, then the Bayer
  threshold, drawn as #aaaaaa dots on a transparent ground and saved as lossless WebP.
