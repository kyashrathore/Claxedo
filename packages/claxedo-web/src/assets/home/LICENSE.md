# Home page assets

## daniell-taj-dither.webp

A 1-bit ordered dither (8 × 8 Bayer, 960 × 609) of **"The Taje Mahel, Agra"**, an
aquatint drawn and engraved by Thomas and William Daniell for *Oriental Scenery*,
1801, held by the British Library.

- Source file: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Daniell_Taje_Mahel3.JPG
  (the British Library's online gallery image,
  http://www.bl.uk/onlinegallery/onlineex/apac/other/019pzz000000395u00000000.html)
- Licence: public domain, tagged `{{PD-art|PD-old-100}}` on Wikimedia Commons: a
  faithful reproduction of a two-dimensional work whose authors died more than 100
  years ago.
- Made from the 922 × 585 Commons file: greyscale, autocontrast (1 % cut), an
  unsharp mask (radius 18, 170 %), a tone curve (black 25, white 250, gamma 1.25),
  then the Bayer threshold, drawn as #aaaaaa dots on a transparent ground and saved
  as lossless WebP.

## plate-*.webp

The section panels use the same treatment as the hero plate, applied by the same
script to six more *Oriental Scenery* aquatints by Thomas and William Daniell: the
source cropped out of its margins, greyscale, autocontrast (1 % cut), a tone curve
(black 25, white 250, gamma 1.25), resized (qutb 480 wide, the rest 640), an unsharp
mask (radius 18, 170 %), then the 8 × 8 Bayer threshold, drawn as #aaaaaa dots on a
transparent ground and saved as lossless WebP. Every source is public domain, as its
Wikimedia Commons record states.
