# WorkGraph Deferred Scope

The following capabilities build on the personal-first service in [SPEC.md](./SPEC.md).

## Collaborative mutation

Initial sharing is owner-controlled and read-only. Shared editing, shared execution authority, ownership transfer, and conflict resolution require a separate collaboration contract.

## Additional authoring adapters

Docs v2 is the initial authoring adapter for appending exact external revisions through the Work Source port. Later authoring integrations use the same source identity, content hash, revision provenance, strict planning, and owner-confirmation contract.

## Organization planning

Company goals, manager-assigned work, organization-wide Streams, and top-down portfolio execution are outside WorkGraph's personal operating-system identity.

## Advanced execution placement

Cost-aware, region-aware, model-performance-aware, and policy-driven placement may extend explicit Work Item execution profiles after local, worktree, user-hosted, and cloud VM execution is stable.

## Templates and marketplaces

Reusable Stream/Outcome templates, loadout marketplaces, and shared operating patterns require versioning, review, provenance, and safe installation contracts.

## Durable external waits

Time, webhook, approval, and provider-event suspension can extend Decisions and triggers after Attempt reconciliation and Recaps are production-ready.

## Additional storage adapters

The initial portability promise is the public contract and conformance suite. Maintained adapters beyond Convex and SQLite follow operator demand.

## Analytics warehouse

Operational storage remains optimized for personal state and realtime use. Aggregate cost, quality, and throughput analytics may project versioned events into a separate system with explicit user consent.
