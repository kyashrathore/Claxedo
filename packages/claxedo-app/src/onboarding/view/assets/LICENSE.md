# Onboarding assets

## daniell-taj-dither.webp

The marketing site's hero plate, byte for byte (`packages/claxedo-web/src/assets/home/daniell-taj-dither.webp`): a 1-bit 8 × 8 Bayer dither (960 × 609, #aaaaaa dots on a transparent ground) of **"The Taje Mahel, Agra"**, an aquatint by Thomas and William Daniell for *Oriental Scenery*, 1801, held by the British Library.

- Source file: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Daniell_Taje_Mahel3.JPG
- Licence: public domain, tagged `{{PD-art|PD-old-100}}` on Wikimedia Commons.
